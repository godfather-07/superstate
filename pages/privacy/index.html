Privacy Policy

what we collect, why, and what we never do with it.

the short version
  • we collect only what we need to deliver your order and talk to you
  • your card and UPI details go to Razorpay; we never see or store them
  • we never sell your personal data

who we are

SuperState ("we", "us") runs superstate.in and sells Sleep Well. If you have any question about your data, contact us on WhatsApp 95673 09388 or email support@superstate.in.

what we collect

  • Order details: your name, WhatsApp number, email, delivery address, pincode, city and state, the pack you chose, and any discount or creator code you used.
  • Payment details: processed by Razorpay. We receive a payment confirmation and payment ID, never your full card number, CVV or UPI PIN.
  • Website usage: pages visited and actions like viewing the product, starting checkout or completing a purchase, collected through cookies and similar tools (Meta Pixel and Google Tag Manager).
  • Messages: anything you send us on WhatsApp or email.

how we use it

  • To process, ship and support your order, including WhatsApp updates and tracking.
  • To credit the creator whose code or link you used.
  • To understand what's working on our website and in our ads, and to show relevant ads on Meta and Google platforms.
  • To respond to your questions, returns and refunds, and to meet legal and tax obligations.

who we share it with

We share data only with the services that help us run the business: Razorpay (payments), our courier partners (delivery), Google (order records and analytics), Meta (ad measurement), and Vercel (website hosting). We do not sell your personal data.

cookies and tracking

We use the Meta Pixel and Google Tag Manager to measure visits and purchases. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and use ad-preference settings on Meta and Google to limit personalised ads.

how long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as needed to serve you and to meet accounting and legal requirements, and then delete or anonymise them.

your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your personal data, or to stop contacting you, at any time via WhatsApp 95673 09388 or email support@superstate.in.

changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date below.
