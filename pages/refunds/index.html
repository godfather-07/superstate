Returns & Refunds

if you don't love it, we'll make it right.

the short version
  • 10-day no-questions-asked returns from the date of delivery
  • refunds go back to your original payment method (UPI or card)
  • damaged or wrong item? we replace or refund it

our promise

We want you to love Sleep Well. If you don't, you can ask for a return within 10 days of delivery, no questions asked.

how to request a return or refund

  • Message us on WhatsApp 95673 09388 or email support@superstate.in within 10 days of delivery.
  • Share your order number (or the phone number/email you ordered with).
  • We'll confirm the next steps, including how to send the pack back if we need it.

how refunds are paid

Once your refund is approved, we initiate it to your original payment method (the UPI account or card you paid with) through our payment partner Razorpay. Banks usually take 5–7 working days to show the money in your account after we initiate it.

damaged, wrong or missing items

If something arrives damaged, the wrong item is delivered, or part of your order is missing, tell us within 48 hours of delivery with photos. We'll send a replacement or a full refund, your choice.

cancellations

Changed your mind before your order ships? Message us and we'll cancel it and refund you in full. Once it has shipped, the return process above applies.

free gifts and discounts

Refunds are for the amount you actually paid, after any discount code. Free gifts that came with an order don't need to be returned.
