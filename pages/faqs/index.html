🌙the basics

What is Sleep Well?
A dark chocolate drink for your evenings. Stir one sachet into 150 ml of hot water and sip it about 30 minutes before bed. Think of it as coffee, but for sleep.
Is it a medicine or a sedative?
No. Sleep Well is a food product, not a medicine. It has no melatonin and no sedatives. It's a nightly ritual, not a knock-out pill.
What makes SuperState different?
No proprietary blends, ever. Every ingredient and its exact amount is on the product page and printed on the pack, so you can check everything yourself.
What does it taste like?
Like a rich dark hot chocolate, because the base is real organic raw cacao, not "chocolate flavour". Made with 150 ml of hot water it's light and chocolatey; with milk it's creamier.
Does it have caffeine?
Cacao naturally contains a small amount of caffeine, far less than a cup of tea or coffee. If you're very sensitive to caffeine, check the pack and start on a night when you're relaxed.

🧪ingredients & safety

What's inside, exactly?
Per 10 g sachet: organic raw cacao 3,000 mg · glycine 3,000 mg · magnesium L-threonate 2,000 mg · magnesium bisglycinate 1,000 mg · tart cherry extract 500 mg · L-theanine 200 mg · apigenin (from chamomile) 50 mg. That's it.
Why two kinds of magnesium?
Different forms of magnesium behave differently, so we used two well-known ones, L-threonate and bisglycinate, instead of one cheap form. Both amounts are on the label.
Why no melatonin?
We wanted a ritual you can enjoy every night, not a hormone shortcut. So Sleep Well is built from food-based ingredients like cacao, glycine, magnesium and chamomile extract.
What about allergens?
The full ingredient and allergen list is printed on the pack. If you have a food allergy, please read it before your first cup.
Who should check with a doctor first?
Please talk to your doctor first if you're pregnant or breastfeeding, take prescription medicine, or have a medical condition. Sleep Well is not for children.
Where is it made?
In Coimbatore, India.

☕using it

How do I have it?
Stir one sachet into 150 ml of hot water (or warm milk, if you like it creamier) and drink it about 30 minutes before bed. Dim the lights, put the phone down, and let it be your wind-down cue.
How much should I have?
One sachet a day. Please don't have more than that.
Can I have it every night?
Yes, that's the idea. It's designed as a nightly ritual, one sachet a night.
When will I notice a difference?
Everyone's different. Most people treat it as a habit rather than a one-night fix, so give it a couple of weeks of having it nightly at the same time.
Water or milk?
The serving size is one 10 g sachet in 150 ml of hot water. If you prefer it creamier, warm milk or plant milk works too. Hot mixes best.
