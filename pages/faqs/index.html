A. The basics

What is Sleep Well? +
A dark chocolate drink mix for your evenings. You stir one sachet into warm milk or water and sip it about 30 minutes before bed. Think of it as coffee, but for sleep.
Is it a medicine or a sedative? +
No. Sleep Well is a food product, not a medicine. It contains no melatonin and no sedatives.
What makes SuperState different? +
No proprietary blends. Every ingredient and its exact amount is listed on the product page and printed on the pack.
What does it taste like? +
Like a rich dark chocolate drink.

B. Ingredients & safety

What's in it? +
Organic raw cacao, glycine, magnesium L-threonate, magnesium bisglycinate, tart cherry extract, L-theanine and apigenin (from chamomile). See the exact amount of each on the product page.
What about allergens? +
The full ingredient and allergen list is printed on the pack. If you have a food allergy, please check it before use.
Who should check with a doctor first? +
Please speak to your doctor before using Sleep Well if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, take prescription medicine, or have a medical condition. Not for children.
Where is it made? +
In Coimbatore, India.

C. Using it

When and how do I have it? +
Stir one sachet into a cup of warm milk or water and drink it about 30 minutes before bed.
How much should I have? +
One sachet a day. Please don't have more than that.

D. Orders & support

How much does shipping cost? +
Shipping is free across India. Orders dispatch in 24–48 hours and usually arrive in 5–6 business days.
Do you offer cash on delivery? +
Yes, COD is available across most pincodes in India. You can also pay by UPI or card.
What is your return policy? +
We offer a 10-day no-questions-asked return policy. If you don't love it, we'll refund you.
I have another question. +
Message us on WhatsApp or email support@superstate.in. A real person replies.
