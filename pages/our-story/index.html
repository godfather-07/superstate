our story

health was never meant to be this complicated.

so we're here to untangle it. here's why SuperState exists.

01 · how we got here

first they sold us the problem. then the "cure".

packaged food got sweeter, snacks got bigger, and convenience won. a lot of us ended up heavier, more tired, and sleeping worse than ever.

then the same kind of big FMCG and supplement brands showed up with the fix: a shiny tub, a big promise on the front, and a label nobody can actually read.

02 · the trick

big on the front. pixie dust inside.

it has a name in the industry: pixie dusting. add a pinch of a fancy ingredient, just enough to print it in big letters on the front, then fill the rest with cheap stuff.

use the cheapest form of an ingredient. hide amounts behind a "proprietary blend". make it cheap to produce, sell it at a premium, and hope nobody flips the pack over.

  • pixie-dusted doses
  • cheap forms
  • hidden blends
  • fine print
03 · what they're counting on

you're not dumb. you're busy.

you've got work, family, a life. nobody has the time to research every ingredient, every form, every dose. and a lot of brands know that.

they're not betting on you being foolish. they're betting on you being tired. that's the part that made us angry.

"they'll never flip the pack over."

04 · so we decided to fix it

we're here to destroy the complexity.

no pixie dust, no hidden blends, no fine print. if it's in the box, you'll see exactly how much, and why it's there, in words a normal person can understand.

  • every dose on the label
  • forms we can explain
  • zero proprietary blends
  • one simple habit
05 · and one more thing

don't trust us blindly either.

we're humans too. we'll make mistakes. so scrutinize us: read our label, question our doses, compare us with anyone, and point out where we got it wrong.

if you genuinely care about your health, we'll be as transparent as you want us to be. ask us anything, and we'll answer in plain words.
