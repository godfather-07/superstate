AN OPEN LETTER

Making health simple again.

Hi there,

Health has been made incredibly complicated by huge FMCG companies and large brands just to sell more of their products.

They have created an environment that is complex and confusing. Between proprietary blends, hidden dosages, and aggressive marketing, it gets completely overwhelming for you, the consumer, to choose and get a product. This constant noise has made following and leading a healthy life really hard to sustain.

I started SuperState because I wanted a brand that makes health simple again.

We believe in putting out the right information and really proving what is inside our products rather than just throwing empty claims. No hidden formulas, no cheap synthetic fillers, and no proprietary secrets.

Our commitment is to be 100% transparent and honest, giving you the raw, clean, and clinically studied building blocks you need to lead a healthier life—one that is easy to sustain and built on trust.

Georgy Varghese
Founder, SuperState
