ANY QUESTIONS?

We have direct answers.

What exactly is Dark Mode? +
Dark Mode is a premium, open-label nighttime functional cocoa powder formulation containing Magnesium L-Threonate, Bisglycinate, L-Theanine, Apigenin, and Glycine. It acts as a delicious nightly dark chocolate ritual designed to switch off racing brain circuits and relax physical muscle cells.
Why fermented dark chocolate/raw cocoa base? +
Cocoa is an elegant, organic carrier molecule. It is rich in natural tryptophan (the core building block of serotonin and melatonin) and mood-enhancing flavonoids. Using it as a warm beverage ritual triggers standard sensory relaxation signals, making it far superior to clinical pill-popping.
Is Magnesium L-Threonate better than standard salts? +
Yes, absolutely. Standard magnesium salts (like oxide or citrate) are poorly absorbed and mostly trigger digestive purging. L-Threonate is the only form scientifically proven to cross the blood-brain barrier directly, rapidly increasing cellular magnesium density inside the central nervous system.
Will it cause chemical dependency? +
No. Unlike melatonin or prescription sleep sedatives, Dark Mode does not introduce synthetic hormones or block receptors. It only supplies natural minerals and amino acids your nervous system naturally utilizes to relax, ensuring zero chemical tolerance or withdrawal issues.
When should I take it? +
We recommend preparing your Dark Mode chocolate ritual exactly 30 minutes before your planned sleep window. This gives the L-Theanine and Magnesium Glycinate plenty of time to drop muscle tension and transition brainwaves into relax mode.
Are there any synthetic ingredients or fillers? +
None. Every dose is strictly unadulterated. We use unrefined low-GI coconut sugar for a light sweetness, 100% organic raw cacao, trace Himalayan sea salts, and direct active elements. There are no preservatives, anti-caking agents, or colorings.
